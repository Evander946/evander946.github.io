About

Who I am

I started as a SQL developer tuning slow queries and stored procedures, and I've stayed close to the data ever since. Over five years I've moved from writing reports to owning whole pipelines: reading the requirement, drawing the design, building it, testing it, taking it through UAT, releasing it and fixing it when something breaks at 2 a.m.

Most of my work has been modernisation. I've rewritten Ab Initio ETL graphs in PySpark, designed a Bronze/Silver/Gold lakehouse from scratch for a marketing platform, and I'm now re-engineering a Java middleware layer into Glue, PySpark, Athena and Iceberg for a bank.

I work comfortably in regulated environments where access control, encryption, schema management and data quality are not optional, and I like sitting with client architects and business teams until a vague requirement becomes a design someone can build.
